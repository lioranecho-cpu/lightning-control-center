Strategy: full channels get a low fee first so traffic can leave. A channel that stays full at a low fee (≤50 ppm) for 7 days with nothing routed out is a recycle candidate: a cooperative close returns your sats for just the on-chain fee, far cheaper than a Loop Out (~0.35%). Channels with a high peer fee are only flagged for closing when they had no traffic in 7 days.
⚠️ Closing is permanent and loses that peer. Before recycling, check the channel is not a sender you still need, and that you have a better use for the sats (a Loop In into a busy exit, or a channel to a peer that sends you traffic). LCC never closes channels by itself.
